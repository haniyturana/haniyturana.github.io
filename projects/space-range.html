Space productivity

Sales, profit and shelf-space share

A brand does not automatically deserve more space simply because sales share exceeds space share. Profit, category role, stock turn, availability and substitution risk must also be considered.

Decision logic

Recommended actions

ExpandHigh sales and profit productivity with insufficient shelf space.
RetainBalanced contribution and strategically important core assortment.
ReduceAcceptable demand but weak productivity relative to space occupied.
Review / delistLow sales, low margin, duplication and excessive stock cover.
Urban premium recommendationIncrease premium skincare depth while retaining essential opening price points. Protect space for high-margin specialist products but cap duplicated variants.
