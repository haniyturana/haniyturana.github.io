Retail performance intelligence case study

Jewellery Retail Performance & Campaign Intelligence

An interactive decision-support dashboard connecting commercial performance, inventory health and campaign returns across physical outlets and e-commerce channels.

Decision objectiveMove from a top-line performance result to its outlet, channel, collection and SKU-level causes—and then to a practical action.
Independent portfolio demonstrationAll transactional, customer, outlet, inventory and campaign data in this case study is synthetically generated. It does not represent any actual company, customer or achieved result.

Portfolio description

One management view across stores and digital commerce

The dashboard provides a unified view of sales, profit, margin, orders, units, average order value, target achievement and inventory exposure across a multi-outlet jewellery business and its online channels. It is designed to answer not only what changed, but why it changed and what the business should review next.

Executive summary

The commercial measures required for a fast portfolio health check.

01Sales, profit and gross margin
02Orders, units and average order value
03Target achievement by outlet and channel
04Inventory value and exposure

Performance diagnosis

Outlet, channel and collection

Compares performance across physical outlets, online channels and product collections to isolate where growth, margin pressure or target gaps originate.

SKU economics

Price, cost, mix and margin

Examines pricing and cost changes, product mix, margin contribution and sales velocity to distinguish volume-led movement from changes in unit economics.

Inventory condition

Availability and allocation

Highlights dead and slow-moving stock, stockout risk, listing status, inventory condition and uneven stock allocation across locations and channels.

Exception control

Products requiring attention

Surfaces negative-margin products and other commercial exceptions so teams can investigate material risks before taking action.

Decision outputs

Translate diagnosis into a prioritised operating response.

Replenish

Restore availability where demand and margin support additional stock.

Transfer stock

Rebalance inventory from weak locations to outlets or channels with stronger demand.

Review pricing

Investigate negative or deteriorating margin caused by price, cost or discount changes.

Clear ageing stock

Prioritise controlled clearance for dead and slow-moving inventory.

Fix operations

Follow up listing, availability and inventory-condition exceptions.

Campaign measurement

Measure incremental performance against a relevant baseline.

Commercial responseSales uplift
Value createdIncremental margin
Range contributionProduct mix
Return measureCampaign ROI

Campaign periods are compared with relevant baselines while product contribution and stock availability are reviewed alongside uplift. This helps separate campaign response from execution constraints and protects the ROI interpretation from top-line sales alone.

Analytical workflow

From connected retail data to governed action

1. ConnectBring together transaction, customer, outlet, inventory and campaign data.
2. ValidateCheck commercial definitions, coverage and data quality.
3. SummariseBuild executive performance and inventory KPIs.
4. DiagnoseDrill through outlet, channel, collection and SKU drivers.
5. CompareEvaluate campaign periods against relevant baselines.
6. RecommendRoute exceptions to replenishment, transfer, pricing, clearance or follow-up.

Conclusion

Use commercial performance and inventory evidence together.

The dashboard frames sales, profitability, stock and campaigns as connected decisions. Recommendations remain review points for management rather than automated instructions, with synthetic data used throughout the demonstration.
