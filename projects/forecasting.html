Forecasting and inventory case study

Retail Demand Forecasting & Inventory Decision Engine

Converting SKU-level demand forecasts into practical stock, safety-stock and reorder recommendations.

Executive summaryForecast accuracy is translated into service-level, lead-time and inventory decisions rather than treated as an isolated model score.
Illustrative analytical demonstrationThis independent portfolio case study uses synthetic data. Results do not represent production accuracy, actual employer performance or a financial forecast.

Business problem

Accuracy alone does not put the right stock in the right place.

Demand changes with seasonality, promotions, pricing, payday periods, product lifecycle, availability and channel behaviour. Forecasts become valuable only when uncertainty is converted into safety stock, reorder points and executable replenishment actions.

The decision engine asks what demand is expected, which SKUs risk stock-out or excess, how service level and lead time change inventory requirements, and which drivers explain the outlook.

Decision formulas

Lead-time demand

Average daily forecast × supplier lead-time days.

Safety stock

Forecast uncertainty × service-level factor × square root of lead time.

Reorder point

Lead-time demand + safety stock.

Recommended order

Maximum of zero and forecast requirement + safety stock − current stock.

Methodology

Forecast, quantify uncertainty and convert it into action

1. Prepare historyValidate demand and availability.
2. Engineer driversBuild price, promotion and calendar features.
3. Compare modelsBenchmark suitable forecasting methods.
4. Time-testValidate on future holdout periods.
5. Quantify uncertaintyEstimate error and service-level buffers.
6. Decide and monitorTranslate outputs, monitor drift and retrain.
