Operational intelligence case study

Enterprise Inventory Reconciliation & Exception Intelligence

Separating genuine stock loss from timing, allocation, status and integration discrepancies across operational systems.

Executive summaryA net variance does not automatically mean physical loss. Exceptions are classified, financially prioritised and validated before adjustment.
Illustrative analytical demonstrationThis independent case study uses synthetic cross-system records and does not represent any employer’s actual inventory or operational performance.

Business problem

Different systems can be correct at different moments.

ERP, OMS, WMS and warehouse records may use different cut-offs, statuses or movement timing. Reconciliation must identify where the quantity differs, whether allocation or posting explains it, and which exceptions require physical validation.

The objective is to correct processes and integrations before considering write-off, while prioritising exposure by quantity, value, age and recurrence.
